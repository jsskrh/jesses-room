I serve as Chief Technology Officer for I&I Exchange BDC, a Central Bank of Nigeria licensed Bureau de Change within the Mitsun Group. I own the technology function end to end, from architecture and delivery through to regulatory systems integration. Some of my key responsibilities include:

- Led the full platform build across a structured 24-week delivery plan, taking the customer web application and the internal staff and compliance console into live production.
- Architected the backend on NestJS and TypeScript with PostgreSQL and Prisma, deployed on infrastructure configured with a reserved static IP to meet financial network whitelisting requirements.
- Designed and implemented the disbursement engine and the double-entry ledger underpinning all customer transactions.
- Built the regulatory reporting layer for the Central Bank of Nigeria, including FIFX returns 217, 305 and 315, validated against the regulator’s published XSD schemas.
- Delivered KYC and identity verification workflows integrating NIBSS BVN and NIN services, alongside card issuance through Sudo Africa and settlement through SafeHaven MFB.
- Directed technology budgeting, partner and vendor assessment, and regulatory impact reviews against the CBN Foreign Exchange Manual and the BDC Guidelines.
- Produced executive and board-level reporting on delivery progress, trade performance, and compliance status.
