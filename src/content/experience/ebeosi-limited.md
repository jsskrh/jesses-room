I worked as a software engineer for Ebeosi Limited, an e-commerce platform which focused on advertising goods based on its place of origin. Some of my key responsibilities here include:

- Developed and optimized client-facing e-commerce applications using JavaScript, TypeScript, and React.js, adhering to a mobile-first design philosophy.
- Designed, built, and consumed RESTful APIs and microservices to support new platform features and integrations.
- Collaborated with product and marketing teams to align technical development with go-to-market (GTM) strategies, translating business requirements into functional features.
- Improved the Continuous Integration (CI) process by updating and streamlining integration scripts, reducing deployment errors and time.
- Implemented websites and landing pages from technical specifications and UX/UI mockups through to deployment, ensuring cross-browser compatibility and responsiveness.
- Maintained and debugged production code, gathered user feedback, and iterated on features to enhance user experience and platform stability.
