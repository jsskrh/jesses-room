## The product

Proxze is a decentralised micro-tasking platform for real-time, location-based verification, logistics audits and data collection. It's built on geofencing, tiered routing and geo-tagged evidence capture.

## What I did

I lead the Proxze product team at Sage Grey Technologies, the venture studio arm of Sage Grey Finance.

- Led development end to end, from requirements gathering and system design to deployment and post-launch support, across web and mobile.
- Delivered four product surfaces: Proxze Retail, Proxze Business, Proxze Enterprise and Proxze Channels.
- Built on the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React, Node.js), integrating databases and third-party APIs.
- Authored and maintain the technical documentation for the system architecture, API specifications and operational procedures.
- Run sprint planning, delivery tracking and reporting for a distributed engineering team.
